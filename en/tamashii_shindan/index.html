About the creator

Michiyo
Spiritual Counsellor / Writer / Messenger

This experience offers a gentle invitation to listen to your own heart, rather than handing you an answer.

House of the Soul brings three doors together: Your Calling, the Starseed exploration I discuss on YouTube, and my own Geometric Cosmic Numerology. I also made this app myself through conversations with AI.

For many years, I have shared the unseen world of energy, vibration, and attraction in the language of everyday life. I have written ten books on attraction, relationships, money, self-love, and angels, and listened alongside many people through my blog, audio programmes, and courses.

What matters to me is the soul’s independence. I see readings and quizzes as tools for recognising your own light, rather than handing your answers to someone else. I hope this experience offers a little window through which you can return to who you are today.

If you would like to explore more of my approach to spirituality, you are warmly welcome here too (in Japanese).

Geometric Cosmic Numerology

Geometric Cosmic Numerology is Michiyo’s approach to divination through the meanings of numbers in sacred geometry and Kabbalah.

It combines the numbers of sacred geometry, understood here as expressing universal truths, traditional Kabbalistic numerology, and Michiyo’s spiritual readings. Through these meanings, it explores the qualities and basic themes the soul has encoded for its life on Earth.
